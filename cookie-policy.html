This Cookie Policy explains how natashacaceres.com (the “Site”) uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with Article 22 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the guidance of the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). It complements our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as the browser’s local storage, work in a comparable way.

2. What this Site uses

No analytics or advertising cookies. The Site does not track your visits or build profiles.

Language preference. When you choose English or Spanish, the Site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage (name “nc-lang”) so the next page opens in the same language. It does not identify you and stays on your device until you clear it. Because it only remembers a choice you made, it does not require consent.

Security. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the Site against malicious traffic (for example “__cf_bm”, which lasts 30 minutes). It does not require consent.

3. Third parties

The typefaces of the Site are served by Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when the page loads, but no cookies are set. When you follow a link to another website, such as Gumroad to buy a report, that website’s own cookie policy applies.

4. How to manage them

Because the Site only uses what is strictly necessary, there is no cookie banner. You can delete cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings.

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Settings, Privacy.
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions.

5. Changes

If we ever add cookies that require consent, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: caceresg.natasha@gmail.com

1. Qué son las cookies

Las cookies son pequeños archivos que un sitio web almacena en su navegador. Las tecnologías similares, como el almacenamiento local del navegador, funcionan de manera comparable.

Ninguna cookie analítica ni publicitaria. El Sitio no rastrea sus visitas ni elabora perfiles.

Cuando usted elige inglés o español, el Sitio guarda esa elección en el almacenamiento local de su navegador (con el nombre «nc-lang») para que la siguiente página se abra en el mismo idioma. Como el Sitio solo utiliza lo estrictamente necesario, no hay banner de cookies. Usted puede eliminar las cookies y el almacenamiento local en cualquier momento desde la configuración de su navegador.

1. Què són les galetes

Les galetes són petits fitxers que un lloc web emmagatzema al seu navegador. Les tecnologies similars, com l'emmagatzematge local del navegador, funcionen de manera comparable.

2. Què utilitza aquest Lloc

Cap galeta analítica ni publicitària. El Lloc no rastreja les seves visites ni elabora perfils.
