Last updated: 22 October 2026Última actualización: 22 de octubre de 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how natashacaceres.com (the “Site”) collects, uses and protects personal data. It complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and Digital Rights (LOPD-GDD).

1. Data controller

Natasha Cáceres, self-employed professional registered in Spain.

  • Tax ID (NIF): 61759305G
  • Address: Gran Via de les Corts Catalanes, 08013 Barcelona, Spain
  • Email: caceresg.natasha@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

Advisory request form. Name, work email, organisation, role, type of engagement, timeline, budget range and the project details you choose to share.

Course enrolment (Open Finance 101). Name, email and professional profile.

Waitlist (Open Finance 201). Name, email, organisation, role and any comment you choose to add.

Purchases. If you buy a report, the purchase takes place on Gumroad, which shares with us your name, email and purchase details.

Technical data. When you visit the Site, your browser’s IP address and basic technical information reach our hosting provider and the font provider (see section 7). We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

3. Why we use your data

To answer requests sent through the forms. To give you access to the courses you enrol in and send you the related emails. To send you occasional updates about new modules, research or reports, when you have agreed to it at enrolment, and you can stop them at any time. To deliver reports you purchase. To comply with legal, accounting and tax obligations. To keep the Site secure.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, for course enrolment, the waitlist and occasional updates. Steps taken at your request and the performance of a contract, for advisory requests, consulting engagements and purchases. Legal obligation, for invoicing and tax records. Legitimate interest, for the security of the Site.

5. How long we keep your data

Messages from the forms are kept for up to 24 months. Course and waitlist data are kept while you remain enrolled or until you ask us to delete them. Data linked to invoices and contracts is kept for the period required by Spanish tax law (6 years).

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to caceresg.natasha@gmail.com. We reply within one month. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

7. Service providers and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. We work with these providers:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), hosting and security of the Site. Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver and protect the Site and is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives the forms sent from the Site and forwards them to our email. It operates outside the European Union.
  • Google (Gmail), where the messages from the forms are stored. Google Ireland Limited, with transfers to Google LLC under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google Fonts, Google LLC (United States), which serves the typefaces of the Site and receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Gumroad, Inc. (United States), which processes report purchases and payments under its own privacy policy.

Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. In the case of FormSubmit, by sending a form you agree to your data being transferred for this purpose.

8. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the law or our services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

For any question about your personal data, write to caceresg.natasha@gmail.com.
